How to Use Language Lips

A short guide to every part of the program — the panel, the buttons, the languages, and how the scoring works.

Before You Start

This is not a pressurised course. There are no time limits, no deadlines, and nothing to finish. Language Lips is a set of practical English lessons you use at your own pace — try one phrase today, come back next week, skip around between sectors. There's no wrong way to use it.

1 Panel Navigation

The left-hand panel is where every lesson lives.

Each sector page is organised into levels — Words, Phrases, Sentences, Dialogues, and more, depending on the sector. Click a level's heading to open or close it.

Inside each level is a list of individual items to practise. Click any word, phrase, sentence, or line of dialogue to bring it up on the main screen.

There's no set order — jump straight to whatever's useful to you.

2 Choosing Words, Phrases & Sentences

Once you've clicked something in the panel:

The mouth on screen speaks it aloud automatically, and the text appears in the caption box underneath.

Click Try it and repeat it back — see the buttons section below for how that's scored.

3 The Three Buttons

Every sector page has the same three controls under the mouth.

🎙 Try it

Listens to you say the phrase currently on screen and compares it to the target text word by word, allowing for near-miss pronunciation. It marks which words came through clearly and which didn't, so you know exactly what to work on — not just a pass/fail score.

🔁 Hear model

Plays the correct pronunciation again, as many times as you like, so you always have something to compare yourself against.

🗣 Free Talk

Say anything you like — it's written on screen as you speak, then read back to you and translated into whichever language is selected in the nav bar. No target text, no score — just open practice.

4 Languages

Along the top of every sector page.

Click any language in the top bar at any time — before, during, or after using a lesson — to see (and hear, in Free Talk) a translation alongside the English.

Translations are there to help you understand — the lesson itself is always about speaking the English text out loud.

5 Sectors Navigation

Switching between the eleven practice modules.

Use the Home button in the top bar to return to the sector list at any time.

Or jump directly between sectors using the links at the bottom of any page — General, Business, Medical, Retail, Food & Beverage, Hospitality, Office, IT, Logistics, Academic, and Travel.

Not sure where to start? General English is a broad mix that works for anyone.
